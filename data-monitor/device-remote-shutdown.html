Important Warning: Do not submit a shutdown request after manually switching off the FatPipe device. This may damage the device and can cause a permanent disconnection between the branch and the Head-End device.
Proper Shutdown Procedure: After initiating the remote shutdown request and receiving confirmation, wait a few minutes for the device to shut down properly. Once all the device LEDs have turned off, switch off the power supply to the FatPipe device. This step is essential for a safe shutdown.
